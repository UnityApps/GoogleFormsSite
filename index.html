FormCreator is a mobile form builder for iPhone that lets you create professional forms, surveys, and questionnaires in minutes — with automatic Google Drive sync, built-in analytics, and data export.

Form Builder
  • Drag-and-drop editor with sections
  • 12 question types: text, multiple choice, dropdown, scale, date, time, number, email, URL, file upload, and more
  • Required/optional questions, hints, images, and YouTube videos
Sharing
  • Unique link for each form
  • Share via messengers, email, and social media
  • Preview before publishing
Analytics & Responses
  • Summary with charts and response statistics
  • Detailed per-question analysis
  • Per-respondent view with filtering and sorting
Data Export
  • Export to CSV and Excel (.xlsx)
  • Save to Google Drive
Google Drive Sync
  • Automatic sync of all forms
  • Access forms from any device
  • Data stored on your Google Drive
Personalization
  • Dark and light themes
  • 3 languages: English, Russian, Spanish
  • Custom form background color and cover image
